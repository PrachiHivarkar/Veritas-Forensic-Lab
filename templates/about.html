Implemented Forensic Techniques & Methodology

Cryptographic Hashes

Calculates MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 hashes simultaneously to establish file verification and immutable chain-of-custody.

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Re-compresses images at a controlled JPEG quality (90%) and calculates pixel difference to detect spliced and retouched elements.

EXIF Hardware Reconstruction

Extracts camera make, model, lens model, exposure parameters, and detects signatures of editors like Adobe Photoshop and GIMP.

GPS Geolocation Mapping

Parses EXIF DMS GPS coordinates into decimal latitude/longitude and pinpoints capture location on an interactive OpenStreetMap layer.

SQLite Database Storage

Maintains persistent user accounts, authentication hashes (Werkzeug), and historical investigation logs locally without external DB servers.

Automated Reporting

Generates court-ready PDF documents using ReportLab, structured plain-text files, and machine-readable JSON exports.
